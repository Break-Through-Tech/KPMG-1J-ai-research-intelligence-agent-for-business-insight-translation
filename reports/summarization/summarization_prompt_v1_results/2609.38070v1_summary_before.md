**Research Objective:**  
To develop a simple yet effective signal for quantifying the uncertainty of large‑language‑model (LLM) answers generated via chain‑of‑thought reasoning, and to evaluate whether counting tokens at which two models strongly disagree can serve as a calibrated confidence estimator. [2609.38070v1]

**Methodology:**  
- Introduced **Divergent Token Confidence (DTC)**, which identifies “divergent tokens” along a reasoning trajectory by measuring the Jensen–Shannon divergence (JSD) between the next‑token distributions of two models and flagging tokens whose JSD exceeds a threshold θ.  
- Provided two estimators: **DTC<sub>count</sub>**, which maps the divergent‑token count directly to a confidence score, and **DTC<sub>prod</sub>**, which combines this count with a full‑sequence probability score.  
- Applied DTC in both **white‑box** settings (using the generator’s logits) and **black‑box** settings (using auxiliary models on the generated trajectory) without requiring additional training or altering generation.  
- Compared DTC against probability‑based, verbalized‑confidence, and UQAC baselines across multiple model families and six mathematical benchmarks, measuring calibration with Expected Calibration Error (ECE) (M = 20 bins). [2609.38070v1]

**Key Findings:**  
- The number of divergent tokens is **almost negatively associated** with path accuracy, indicating that more disagreement signals lower answer reliability. [2609.38070v1]  
- In white‑box evaluation, the count‑only estimator achieves an **average ECE of 13.0 %**, substantially better than the **32.7 %–42.4 %** range of standard full‑sequence confidence methods. [2609.38070v1]  
- In black‑box settings, DTC reduces ECE from **32.1 %–40.2 %** (original verbalized scores) to **13.7 %–16.3 %** on the DeepSeek‑V3.2 model. [2609.38070v1]  
- Across all tested model families and benchmarks, DTC improves calibration relative to probability‑based, verbalized‑based, and UQAC baselines, and it also mitigates overconfidence in verbalized scores. [2609.38070v1]
